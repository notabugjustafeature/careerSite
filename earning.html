Job Market and Earning Potential

  • Employment - High demand is expected for information security analysts. Cyberattacks have grown in frequency, and these analysts will be needed to create innovative solutions to prevent hackers from stealing critical information or creating problems for computer networks.
  • Median Pay - $124,910 per year or $60.05 per hour in 2024
  • Job Outlook - Employment of information security analysts is projected to grow 29 percent from 2024 to 2034, much faster than the average for all occupations.
